Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

This Privacy Policy explains what information Novara ("we", "our", "the app") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using Novara, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect a username and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text), or if you use "Continue with Google," we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID from Google.

Content you provide. The messages you send, files you attach (images, PDFs, videos), and any prompts you use for video generation/editing are stored so your conversation history is available when you come back.

Voice input. If you use the microphone button, your speech is converted to text directly by your browser/device — audio is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Technical information. We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate your account and show you your past conversations
  • To generate AI responses (your message and relevant context are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a reply)
  • To run a web search on your behalf when you turn on "Web search," or when your question clearly needs current information
  • To improve reliability and fix problems

3. Third parties we work with

We share the minimum data necessary with these service providers so Novara can function:

  • Google Gemini API — receives your message text (and image, if attached) to generate a reply.
  • Google Sign-In — used only if you choose "Continue with Google," to verify your identity.
  • DuckDuckGo — receives your search query when web search is used, to fetch results.
  • Hosting provider (Render) — hosts our servers and database.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Chat sharing

If you use the "Share" feature on a conversation, a public link is created. Anyone with that link can view that conversation, whether or not they have a Novara account. You can disable sharing at any time from the Share menu, which immediately deactivates the link.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and conversation data until you delete your account. You can request deletion at any time — see Section 7.

6. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never visible to us in plain text. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

7. Your rights — access & deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, conversations, messages, uploaded files) at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

8. Children's privacy

Novara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Novara after a change means you accept the updated policy.
