Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect
Your username or email, and, if you sign in with Google, your name and Google account email. We also store the chats, messages, and files you send to Novara so you can revisit them later.

2. How we use it
To run your account, keep your conversation history available to you, and improve Novara's responses over time.

3. Uploaded files
Images, PDFs, and other files you attach are stored so Novara can reference them in that conversation. Don't upload anything you wouldn't want stored.

4. Sharing
We don't sell your data. If you use the "Share" feature on a chat, anyone with that link can view it — only share chats without sensitive information.

5. Third-party sign-in
If you sign in with Google, we only receive the basic profile info (name, email) needed to create your account.

6. Deleting your data
You can permanently delete your account and all associated chats at any time from Profile & Settings → Delete account.

7. Changes
This policy may be updated as Novara's features change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
